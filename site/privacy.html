Privacy

This website is static. Motus keeps its ledger on your machine.

This website

The site has no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms, accounts, or client-side JavaScript. It never receives your Motus ledger.

GitHub Pages may process standard request data such as your IP address, browser, requested URL, and request time. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The command-line application

Motus stores selected command facts in .motus/ledger.db at the current Git root, or in the current directory outside Git. You can choose another path. Motus does not send the ledger anywhere.

Wrapped-command argument values and stdin, raw stdout and stderr, environment variables, source files, prompts, and transcripts are not stored.

Motus stores finding text and closure notes only when you submit them. They remain until you delete the ledger file.

Your control

The ledger is a local SQLite file. You choose its location, backups, access, and deletion. Add .motus/ to .gitignore if it should stay out of version control.

Motus Work Ledger is open source under Apache-2.0.
